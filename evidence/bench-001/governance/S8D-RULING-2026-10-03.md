## 2. The ruling

1. **§8(d) is reading (ii): top-rank resolution compositionality over the arm's served evidence surface.** The metric's object is the served (ranked) evidence list the arm actually surfaces — exactly what `ChunkSpecHvResolution.scoreQuery` (core `c4b67e8`) has computed on every scored run since r8, fed by each run's served `ranked_refs`.
2. **Granularity (the handoff's open point, closed):** gate arithmetic on the per-query **full-coverage** reading (`G ⊆ C` — the stricter reading of "covers the query's gold spec points"); the per-point **micro-average** is reported beside every run (ruling 1's dual-reporting spirit). Both readings have been recorded in every run report since r8 — this ruling pins which one drives the gate, with no retro-fit of any recorded number.
3. **Horizon:** (d) is scored over the run's configured served surface (top-20 through tranche 3; top-40 from tranche 4 onward). Horizon-sensitivity is the metric working as designed — a carrier one rank past the served horizon is an uncovered point. The regression comparison is against the recorded baseline of the comparison series as pinned by the runs' own reports (r8 = 0.5618 full / 0.9167 micro for the run-005-c lineage — the recovery-ceiling framing per the T-C72 pre-registration's honest different-basis note).
4. **Mapping fidelity stays guarded where it already lives:** the per-run HV drift gate (0 divergences in every recorded generation), the projection census pin (210/209/164/181, asserted fail-closed in every run), and the substrate bridge's anchor-kind audit (205 CLEAN / 4 MULTI / 1 MISS). No new instrument, no metric letter added, no §8.x amendment needed.

## 3. Why — the textual basis (review pack §2, unchanged by anything since)

The spec's own words define (d) over the serving surface, not the mapping layer: §1 names the guarded failure mode as "a retrieval system can produce fluent answers while resolving the wrong SpecificationPoint," with the primary quality axis being "SpecificationPoint resolution and evidence identity"; §5 defines the flagship metric as whether the arm "**surfaces** evidence whose `HUMAN_VALIDATED` spec mapping covers the query's gold spec points"; §8 v1.0(d) is a *regression rule* ("does not regress by more than 1 percentage point"), which presupposes a metric that moves when serving moves — under reading (i) it could not move at all in exactly the scenario §1 built it for, and F5 of the review pack records why that would make (d) uninformative as a gate. The scoring handoff §5 quotes the §5 definition verbatim and specifies the implemented scorer; the implemented scorer and the recorded runs all measure (ii).
