## 1. The lever, exactly

**Single lever: per-arm EXTERNAL_NOTES floor, N = 5, at depth 40** — via a NEW bench env gate `BENCH_NOTES_FLOOR` (integer; default ABSENT → the existing path byte-identical, the S8D absent-path precedent). Semantics, pinned now: per arm, AFTER the limit-40 selection that reproduces the recorded f posture, take the arm's EXTERNAL_NOTES candidates in ARM-RANK order that are not already in that arm's list, up to **N = 5**, and APPEND them to the arm's list (list length 40 + up to 5). Fusion unchanged (the shipped `ReciprocalRankFusion` k=60, unweighted — the 3-arg ctor posture). Both views identically; the boundary applies to the extended lists exactly as recorded; the determinism double-pass covers the extended path.

**Design choice declared BEFORE the run — FLOOR form, not CAP form, and why:** the per-kind quota family admits two shapes. The CAP form (paper kinds capped, notes rise within a fixed 40-list) DISPLACES paper refs — the union set changes both ways, the T-C67 superset invariant and its (d) monotonicity theorem die, and the f→g delta conflates two effects (removal + admission). The FLOOR form is pure-additive: the recorded 40 stay untouched, notes are appended from beyond rank 40 — the union can only grow, the f→g delta is admission ALONE, and the (d) monotonicity theorem (§3.1) extends structurally. The cap form's removal effect is NOT licensed by any recorded evidence of need; if this run's ledger shows the floor insufficient, the cap form gets its own dated pre-registration. One lever, one attribution.

**Why N = 5 (declared now, before the run):** (i) the census shows each missed query needs ≥ 1 admitted covering ref (one ref per missing point — every missed query misses exactly one point); (ii) restoring the r9-era notes SHARE at f's pool size computes to ≈ +2–3 notes refs per query union-wide → floor ≈ 2 per arm is the minimal share-restoring value; (iii) N = 5 doubles that headroom against the unrecorded arm-rank depth of the carriers (arm ranks beyond 40 are NOT recorded anywhere — this prereg cannot know how deep they sit), while union notes stay ≤ r9's recorded maximum range (15–21/query). No sweep: **any other N, or a cap-form variant, = dated new pre-registration + new run.**

**Out of scope for this tranche (each its own future pre-registration):** the cap form (paper-kind caps / composition reshape within the limit), MIN_COSINE floor moves, re-ingest dedup, arm-composition changes, serving-side changes of any kind. This tranche measures pure ADDITIVE notes admission at the recorded depth-40 posture.

## 2. Mechanism analysis (why the floor can reach what depth could not)

Depth 40 grew the pools but the growth was paper-dominated (grounding §0.2): the arm rankers (bm25 / pgvector relevance) order the wave's paper mass above the displaced notes carriers, so deeper LIMITs admit more paper before they reach the notes chunks — notes share FELL at depth 40. The floor bypasses the arm ranking for the notes kind specifically: it admits the arm's first N EXTERNAL_NOTES candidates beyond rank 40 REGARDLESS of their arm rank, which is exactly the displacement the (d) ledger records ("outranked within their arm by paper mass"). The lever is kind-aware in the quota dimension ONLY — no query-kind awareness, no gold knowledge, no per-query tuning (the same N=5 for every query and both arms, pre-registered here).
